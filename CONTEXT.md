# my-site

Personal site. Appearance and Accent are separate: one is light/dark, the other is hue.

## Language

**Accent**:
The site's hue. One of green, red, or blue. Blue is the default.
_Avoid_: theme, color-theme, ThemeChanger, darkBlue

**Appearance**:
Light, dark, or system. How surfaces are painted, not which hue.
_Avoid_: theme (when you mean Accent)
